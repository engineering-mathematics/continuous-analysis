# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

### Added
- `Domain` sampling grid and `Polynomial` arithmetic, calculus and roots.
- `TransferFunction` with poles, zeros, dc gain, stability, interconnections and state-space realisation.
- `StateSpace` with shape validation, poles, stability and transfer-function conversion (SISO).
- `LinearODE` with exact autonomous solutions (matrix exponential) and adaptive forced solutions (DOP853).
